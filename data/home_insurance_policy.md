# Home Insurance Policy — Summary of Cover

## What is covered
This home insurance policy covers loss of or damage to the buildings and contents of the
insured home caused by the following insured events: fire, lightning, explosion, storm,
flood, theft or attempted theft, escape of water from fixed water tanks or pipes
(including damage from a burst pipe), falling trees, and impact by vehicles or aircraft.

## Water damage
Sudden and accidental water damage caused by a burst pipe or a leaking appliance is
covered, including the cost of tracing and accessing the source of the leak. Damage
caused by gradual leakage, lack of maintenance, or wear and tear is NOT covered.

## Contents cover
Household contents are covered up to the sum insured stated in the policy schedule.
High-value items such as jewellery, watches, and electronic equipment are covered up to
a single-item limit of EUR 2,000 unless specified separately on the schedule.

## Excess
For each claim the policyholder pays the first EUR 250 (the "excess"). A higher excess of
EUR 500 applies to claims for escape of water.

## Exclusions
The policy does not cover: damage from wear and tear or lack of maintenance; damage while
the home is unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days; damage from pests or vermin;
and any loss arising from war, terrorism, or nuclear risks.

## Claims process
Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident. The insurer aims to acknowledge a
claim within 5 working days and to settle valid claims within 30 days of receiving all
required documentation.
