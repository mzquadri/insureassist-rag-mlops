# Auto Insurance Policy — Summary of Cover

## Types of cover
Three levels are available: Third-Party Only, Third-Party Fire and Theft, and
Comprehensive. Comprehensive cover includes damage to the policyholder's own vehicle in
addition to third-party liability.

## Comprehensive cover
Comprehensive cover pays for accidental damage to the insured vehicle, fire, theft, and
damage caused by vandalism. It also includes a courtesy car for up to 14 days while the
insured vehicle is being repaired by an approved garage.

## Third-party liability
All policies cover the policyholder's legal liability for injury to other people and
damage to their property, up to EUR 20 million for property damage and unlimited cover for
personal injury.

## Excess
A standard excess of EUR 400 applies to own-damage claims under Comprehensive cover.
Drivers under 25 years old pay an additional young-driver excess of EUR 300.

## No-claims discount
A no-claims discount is earned for each year without a claim, up to a maximum of 65% after
five claim-free years. One claim reduces the discount by two years' worth of steps.

## Exclusions
The policy does not cover: driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs; using the
vehicle for racing or hire and reward unless declared; mechanical or electrical breakdown;
and wear and tear of tyres.

## Claims process
Accidents should be reported within 48 hours. For theft, a police report reference number
is required before the claim can be processed.
